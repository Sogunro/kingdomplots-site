About Kingdom Plots

Kingdom Plots is a Christian platform exploring the relationship between faith, economics, politics and society. Through films, articles, research, debates and games, we help Christians understand the Bible’s view of the economic and social questions that shape everyday life and the wider world.

Our vision

To see Christian faith, values and wisdom meaningfully shape the economic and social life of individuals, communities and nations.

Our mission

To equip Christians to think biblically, engage intelligently and take part confidently in the economic and social issues that shape our world.

Why we exist

We believe Christianity should inform more than personal spirituality. It should also shape how we work, trade, save, give, govern, build families and organise society.

Kingdom Plots exists to explore what that looks like, and to bring biblical wisdom into the economic and social questions of our time.

Our guiding principle

Christianity has something meaningful to say about how society works, not just how individuals worship.

Six ways, one mission

Films

Films that explore Bible passages, stories and principles, and show what they mean for today’s economic and social questions.

Articles

Thoughtful, readable writing on what the Bible teaches about money, business, work, family, government and society.

Research

Surveys and original studies of what Christians believe, how they behave and the challenges they face in economic and social life.

Debate

Different Christian views in conversation on hard economic and social questions, reasoned from the Bible.

Tech and AI

Advice, websites, apps and AI tools for churches, ministries and Christian businesses.

Games

Quizzes, Bible journeys and word searches that help people discover and remember what the Bible says. Play at kingdomplotsgames.com.

How we work

The verse comes first

Every film, article and study starts from a Bible passage and names it.

Both sides are heard

Where Christians disagree, we set out each view fairly before we give ours.

No party lines

On government and nations we say what the Bible says, and stop there.

Checked by people

Every film script, article and research finding is checked by a person before it goes out.
